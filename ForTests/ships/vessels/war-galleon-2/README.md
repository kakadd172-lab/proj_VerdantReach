# War Galleon II — ForTests Ship Design Studio

A separate sibling to the original War Galleon, registered as its own editable
vessel in the standalone ForTests shipyard. The original `war-galleon` model
and its saved defaults remain untouched.

- Design envelope: 38.0 m length, 10.6 m beam and 4.7 m draft.
- Three masts: square-rigged fore and main, lateen mizzen, bowsprit and headsails.
- Independent hull loft, stepped sterncastle, galleries, raised forecastle,
  standing rigging, component mounts, mast stations, defaults and ship-local generators.
- Forward is -Z, aft +Z, port -X, starboard +X, and up +Y.
- A proportional composite reconstruction, not an exact surviving vessel or
  traced historical lines plan.

Edit this vessel under `ForTests/ships/vessels/war-galleon-2/`. Its geometry
algorithms are local to this folder; the shared folder provides editor lifecycle
and definition/override support only.
