# War Galleon

A dedicated late-sixteenth-century Atlantic galleon reconstruction for the standalone ForTests Ship Design Studio.

It has its own hull loft, frames, strakes, deck, low forecastle, narrowing stepped sterncastle, rudder, helm, ordinary deck fittings, three separate mast generators, sail cloth profile, headsails, and standing rigging. It does not import the merchant-galleon generator or borrow another vessel's hull or sail geometry.

The 38.0 m × 10.6 m dimensions are an authored proportional reconstruction, not a claim to reproduce a surviving ship's original lines plan. Historical evidence for individual sixteenth-century designs is incomplete, and unrecorded details are treated as reconstruction choices.

Coordinate system: forward -Z, aft +Z, port -X, starboard +X, up +Y; origin at keel-midships. The first pass is a static editor model; motion and additional rigging detail are intended for later visual review.
